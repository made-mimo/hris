# SI HRIS - User Guide

Welcome to SI HRIS, your company's HR system. This guide shows you how to sign in, find your way around, and use the features you will need most. Everything is done in your web browser, on a computer, tablet or phone.

> **What you see depends on your role.** The menu only shows the screens you are allowed to use. If a feature in this guide is not in your menu, your role does not include it - that is normal. See [Roles](#3-roles-and-what-they-can-see).

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the HRIS web address your company gave you.
2. Enter your **work e-mail (or username)** and **password**, then click **Sign in**.
3. Tick **Remember me** only on a personal device.
4. If your company has switched them on, you will also see **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Microsoft** - use your company account.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the e-mail. If no e-mail arrives, ask your HR Admin to help.

**Two-step verification.** If your account requires it, after your password you will be asked to **Verify it's you** with a 6-digit code - from your authenticator app, or e-mailed to you (use **Resend code** if it does not arrive). On first use you will be walked through setting it up. See [Account security](#18-account-security).

**Password changes.** If the company tightens its password rules, you may be asked to choose a new password the next time you sign in. This is expected.

**Automatic sign-out.** For your protection you are signed out after a period of no activity (set by your company). Unsaved typing is lost, so submit forms before stepping away.

**Signing out.** Click your picture/initials at the top right, then **Sign out**.

## 2. Finding your way around

### On a computer or tablet

- **Left sidebar** - your main menu, grouped into sections. Click a section heading to collapse or expand it; use **Expand all / Collapse all** at the top. The sidebar remembers what you opened and keeps its scroll position as you move between pages.
- **Top bar:**
  - **Search box** (left, on larger screens) - type a name or Employee ID and press Enter to jump to the [Directory](#directory) with your search applied. It appears only if your role includes the Directory.
  - **Help (?)** - opens the help page for the screen you are on.
  - **Sun/moon button** - switch between light and dark mode.
  - **Bell** - your notifications ([see Notifications](#17-notifications)).
  - **Your picture or initials** - **My Profile**, **Change password**, **Sign out**.
- **Page area** - the screen you picked. Most pages have tabs or action buttons at the top.

Sidebar sections (you will only see those your role allows):

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your dashboard |
| **Workspace** | My Leave, My Claims, Travel Advance, My Timesheets, My Attendance, Performance, Helpdesk |
| **My info** | My Info, My Requests |
| **Socials** | Pulse Surveys, Buzz |
| **My team** | Approvals, Timesheet Approvals, Employees, Recruitment, Discipline Cases (for managers and HR) |
| **Company** | Directory, Assets, Vehicles, Policies |
| **HR admin** | Configuration screens for HR |
| **Admin** | Settings, roles, audit log and other system screens |

### On a phone

- A **bottom bar** gives you three quick shortcuts plus **More**. The shortcuts **change to match the section you are in** - for example, inside Workspace you get Leave, Claims and Timesheets; inside My team you get Approvals, Employees and Recruit; inside Socials you get Surveys and Buzz. Shortcuts only appear for screens your role allows.
- Tap **More** to open the full menu. It opens on the section you are currently in. Tapping another section heading opens it and closes the previous one, to save space. Use **Expand all** if you want everything open, and **Collapse all** to tidy up.
- Wide tables can be swiped sideways. The top-bar search box is hidden on small screens - open **Company > Directory** instead.

### Good to know

- Dates are shown **day / month / year**.
- Buttons in **red** are the main action on a form (for example **Choose file**, **Submit**, **Post**).
- A green banner at the top of a page confirms that your action worked; red text under a field tells you what to fix.

## 3. Roles and what they can see

Every user has one base role. Your HR Admin or Admin sets it.

| Role | Who it is for | Main access |
|---|---|---|
| **ESS (Employee)** | Every employee | Your own leave, claims, timesheets, attendance, info, performance, helpdesk tickets, policies; Buzz, Pulse Surveys, Directory; assets/vehicles assigned to you |
| **Supervisor** | Added **automatically** to anyone who has people reporting to them | Everything ESS has, plus **Approvals** for your team |
| **HR Officer** | HR support staff | Approvals, Employees, Recruitment, claims management; no access to compensation or discipline data |
| **HR Admin** | HR leadership | All employee data and all HR configuration screens |
| **Admin** | Platform administrator | Everything, including Settings, Roles & Permissions, Audit Log |

You only ever see records you are allowed to: staff see their own, supervisors see their team's, HR sees everyone's.

- **Balance cards** - annual and sick leave available, your open requests, claims awaiting payment, hours clocked this week.
- **Quick actions** - **Apply for leave**, **Submit a claim**, **Raise a helpdesk ticket**, attendance.

The **My open requests** card takes you to the full list on [My Requests](#8-my-info-and-my-requests).

## 5. Leave

### Apply for leave

1. **Workspace > My Leave** (or **Apply for leave** on Home).
2. Choose the **Leave type**. The number of **days available** is shown.
3. Pick **From** and **To** dates.
4. Choose **Duration per day**: **Full day**, **Half day - AM**, **Half day - PM**, or **Specific time**. The form shows how many working days you are requesting.
5. Optionally pick a **Reliever** (the colleague covering for you) and add a **Reason**.
6. Click **Submit request**.

**What happens next** (shown on the form):

1. Your reliever is notified - no action needed from them.
2. Your **Line Manager** approves or rejects.
3. **HR & Admin** gives final approval; your balance is re-checked at this step.

You are notified at each stage, and can follow it in **My Requests**. You can **cancel** a request while it is pending, or after approval if the leave has not yet been taken. You cannot book overlapping leave.

### Assign leave (HR and managers with Approvals access)

**Approvals > Assign leave** books leave for someone directly as approved, with no approval steps. Entitlement is used immediately and overlap checks still apply. HR may tick **Bypass the balance check** for an exceptional grant.

## 6. Expense claims and travel advances

### Submit a claim

1. **Workspace > My Claims**.
2. Choose the **Claim event** (the trip or activity) and **Currency** (NGN, USD or GBP).
3. Under **Expenses**, set the **Type**, **Date**, a **Note** and the **Amount** for the first line. Click **Add expense** for more lines; the trash icon removes a line. The subtotal updates as you type.
4. A yellow **Above cap** notice means a line exceeds the company limit for that expense type - it can still be submitted but will be reviewed.
5. Optionally attach a **Receipt / supporting document** (see [Uploading files](#19-uploading-files)).
6. Click **Submit claim**. Expenses lock once submitted.

**Approval route:** your Line Manager, then HR & Admin, who route it to Finance for payment. Very large claims need an additional **second approval** from an Admin. When Finance pays, the status becomes **Paid** and it counts under **Claims awaiting payment** on Home until then.

### Travel advance

Need money before a trip? **Workspace > Travel Advance**: choose the **Claim event**, enter the **Amount**, click **Request advance**. When you later submit a claim for the same event, the advance is reconciled against it automatically. Finance confirms payment (with method and reference).

## 7. Timesheets and attendance

### Timesheets (weekly hours on projects)

1. **Workspace > My Timesheets**. Use the **arrows** to move between weeks.
2. Pick a **project** and **activity**, then click **Add row**.
3. Enter hours for each day (Mon-Sun); the **Total** updates. Click **Save hours** as you go.
4. When the week is complete, click **Submit for approval** and confirm. Your manager approves it.
5. If it is **rejected**, the reason is shown; fix the hours and click **Resubmit**. Submitted or approved timesheets cannot be edited.

An **Action log** under the sheet shows who did what and when. Managers approve under **My team > Timesheet Approvals**: **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason.

### Attendance (clock in and out)

- Click **Clock in** on the Home page when you start and **Clock out** when you finish.
- **Workspace > My Attendance** shows your punch history. Where your company allows it you can correct a punch (**Edit**) or add one with a punch-in time. Supervisors may be allowed to punch for their team using the **Viewing** selector.

## 8. My Info and My Requests

### My Info

Open it from the sidebar (**My info > My Info**) or from your picture at the top right.

- **Profile picture** - click **Choose file**, select a photo, then click **Upload**. **Remove** takes it off.
- **Details** - your Employee ID, job title, department, supervisor and hire date (read-only; ask HR to change these).
- **Biodata** - personal details, contact details, qualifications and career development that you can keep up to date yourself. Save each section after editing.

### My Requests

A single list of your leave requests and expense claims with their dates, **Stage** and **Status**. Reach it from the **My open requests** card on Home.

## 9. Approvals (managers and HR)

**My team > Approvals** lists everything waiting for **you**, with tabs **All**, **Leave** and **Claims** and a count on each.

1. Click an item on the left to see its details on the right.
2. Optionally add a **Comment** - it is sent to the employee with your decision.
3. Click the approve button (its label shows the next step, e.g. **Approve**, **Give final approval**, **Approve and route to Finance**) or **Reject**.

A red badge on the menu shows how many items await you. HR Admin can also mark approved claims as paid.

## 10. Helpdesk

Raise and track requests to IT, HR, Facilities and others.

1. **Workspace > Helpdesk > Raise a ticket**.
2. Choose a **Category**, enter a **Subject** and **Description**, and click **Submit**.
3. Click a ticket to read it, add a **comment**, and follow its status: open, in progress, resolved, closed. Use **Reopen** if it is not truly fixed. Tick **My tickets only** to hide others' tickets if you can see more than your own.

**Confidential categories (e.g. Grievance / Whistleblower)** are marked *(confidential)*. They are visible only to you and a restricted list of handlers - not your manager. You may tick **Submit anonymously**: your identity is hidden from handlers unless you are specifically identified for follow-up.

Handlers (HR/IT) can **Start progress**, **Assign** to a colleague, **Mark resolved** and **Close**.

## 11. Buzz, Pulse Surveys and the Directory

### Buzz (company social feed)

Everyone sees every post. At the top choose **Text**, **Photo** or **Video**:

- **Text** - type your message and click **Post**.
- **Photo** - click the red **Choose file** button and select up to 6 images (each up to 10 MB), add an optional caption, then **Post**.
- **Video** - paste a **YouTube or Vimeo link**, optionally add a caption, then **Post**.

You can **Like**, **Comment** and **Reshare** (with a caption), and delete your own posts and comments. The feed refreshes by itself about every 15 seconds, so new posts appear without reloading. **Upcoming work anniversaries** are listed alongside.

### Pulse Surveys

Short, **anonymous** check-ins. Open **Socials > Pulse Surveys**: each open survey shows its closing date and questions - click a number on the rating scale, or type a free-text answer (optional), then **Submit anonymously**. Your individual answers are never shown to anyone; HR only sees combined results, and only when enough people have responded. Surveys you have completed move to **Already responded**.

### Directory

**Company > Directory** is a searchable staff list. Search by **name or Employee ID** (or use the search box in the top bar), or filter by **Job title** and **Location**. Click a person's card to open their details; click again to close it. Only current employees are shown unless you search by name/ID, which also finds former staff.

## 12. Performance

**Workspace > Performance** has four tabs:

- **Reviews** - formal reviews. Managers and HR create a review for a period and rate KPIs, with an overall rating and final comment; you see your own.
- **Goals** - objectives with a target, current value, unit, due date and status. Update progress as you go.
- **Tracker** - an ongoing log of positive and negative notes about performance, dated and with a description.
- **360° Feedback** - feedback cycles where colleagues give input.

What you can see or edit depends on your role: employees see their own, supervisors their team's, HR everyone's.

---

## 13. Policies

**Company > Policies** lists company policy documents by category.

- **Outstanding acknowledgements** appears at the top when a policy needs your confirmation: click **View** to read it, then **I acknowledge and agree**. Each acknowledgement is recorded with the document version.
- Browse by category, **View** or **Download** the current version. A badge shows **Acknowledged** once you have confirmed.
- When a policy is updated to a new version you may be asked to acknowledge again.

HR Admins publish new versions and can see who has acknowledged (**View compliance**).

## 14. Assets, Vehicles and Discipline Cases

### Assets and Vehicles

**Company > Assets** and **Company > Vehicles** show company items **assigned to you** (laptop, phone, car, etc.) with their details. HR and asset managers can add items, **Assign** / **Unassign** them, log maintenance, warranties, fuel and mileage, and record renewals (insurance, licence).

### Discipline Cases

**My team > Discipline Cases**. If a case concerns you, you can read it and use **Respond** to give your side. HR **raises** a case (employee, case type, severity, incident date, description, evidence), may **raise a follow-up** question, and **resolves** it with an outcome. Access is restricted to HR Admin and those directly involved.

## 15. Recruitment and Employees (managers and HR)

### Recruitment

**My team > Recruitment** has three tabs:

- **Requisitions** - a hiring manager requests a new position (title, number of positions, hiring manager, justification); approvers **Approve** or **Reject** with a comment.
- **Vacancies** - approved roles posted for applicants (shown on the public careers page).
- **Candidate Pipeline** - applicants moving through stages for each vacancy, with CVs and offers.

### Employees (HR)

**My team > Employees** is the employee master list.

- Search by name or employee ID; **Show filters** to narrow by status and other fields.
- **Add employee** - only the name is required; the Employee ID is generated automatically and the rest can be completed later. Bulk-add with the **CSV import** (download the template first).
- Click a person to open their record: **Job Details, Personal, Contact, Family, Immigration, Compensation, Qualifications, Onboarding/Offboarding, Career Development, Attachments & Custom Fields, Reporting, Termination, Activity** (some tabs may be switched off by your company).
- **Org chart** shows reporting lines, computed live. **Reports** builds exportable employee reports.

## 17. Notifications

The **bell** in the top bar shows unread alerts - for example a leave decision, a claim update, an approval waiting for you, or a ticket reply. Click an item to go straight to it. **Mark all read** clears the count; **Clear all** removes them.

Click **Enable browser push notifications** in the bell panel to receive alerts even when HRIS is in another tab (your browser will ask permission). You may also receive e-mail notifications.

## 18. Account security

Open your picture at the top right > **Change password**.

- **Change password** - enter your current password, then a new one twice. It must meet your company's password rules (shown if it is too weak), and cannot be an easily-guessed password.
- **Two-step verification** - choose **Set up authenticator app** (scan the QR code with an authenticator app, then enter the 6-digit code to confirm) or **Use email code instead**. You can switch methods any time.
- **Backup codes** - save the codes shown; each works once if you lose your phone. **Regenerate backup codes** replaces them.
- **Trusted devices** - if allowed, you can skip the code on devices you trust; **Revoke** removes a device or **Revoke all**.

Never share your password or codes. Changing your password signs you out of your other browsers.

## 19. Uploading files

Anywhere you attach a file (profile photo, receipts, documents, Buzz photos) you will see a red **Choose file** button.

1. Click **Choose file** and pick the file(s). The file name appears beside the button.
2. Where there is an **Upload**, **Save** or **Submit** button, click it to finish - choosing a file alone does not send it.

Limits: up to **10 MB per file**; Buzz allows up to 6 photos at once. Documents accept PDF/Word; HR attachments also accept Excel and images. If you see "failed to upload", the file is too large or the wrong type - compress it or convert it and try again.

## 20. Tips and troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| A menu item is missing | Your role does not include it. Ask HR if you think you need access. |
| "Not enough balance" on leave | Check available days on the form; ask HR if an adjustment is needed. |
| Cannot edit a timesheet or claim | Submitted/approved items are locked. A rejected timesheet can be edited and resubmitted. |
| Page looks stale | Refresh the browser (most pages update automatically). |
| Cannot sign in | Check Caps Lock, use **Forgot password?**, or ask HR Admin to unlock your account (repeated wrong attempts temporarily lock it). |
| Verification code did not arrive | Click **Resend code**, check spam, or use a backup code. |
| Layout looks wrong on a phone | Rotate the phone, or use **More** for the full menu. |
| Something looks wrong or you found an error | Raise a **Helpdesk** ticket (IT Support) with a short description and the page name. |

For questions about your pay, leave entitlement or records, raise an **HR Request** ticket in Helpdesk.
